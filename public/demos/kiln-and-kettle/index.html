Our story

Started with one oven and a borrowed grinder.

Kiln & Kettle opened in 2021 in a converted garage on Admiralty Way. We wanted a place where the coffee is taken seriously and nobody is rushed.

We buy beans from small farms in Kaduna and Mambilla, roast them every morning behind the counter, and bake with flour milled an hour from the city. Come for a flat white, stay for the jollof eggs.
