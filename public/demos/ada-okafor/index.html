About

I photograph the in-between moments.

The laugh after the vows. The father fixing his agbada before the walk. The quiet at the end of a long street in Accra. Those are the frames people keep coming back to.

I've been shooting for nine years across Nigeria, Ghana and Europe. I work with natural light, a small kit and a lot of patience, so you can forget the camera is there.
